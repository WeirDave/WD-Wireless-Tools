Custom destinations
Add your own destination subfolders alongside the built-in three. Each destination routes files by extension (like the built-ins) and can optionally claim PDFs or JSONs whose filenames contain specific keywords. A custom extension takes precedence over a built-in one — routing .csv to a new raw-data folder overrides the default reports mapping.
