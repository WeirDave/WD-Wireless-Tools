Draw what to keep

Drag a rectangle around what you want to keep. Everything outside it is cut away, like scissors on a sheet — drag the handles to adjust before you save. Hold Space and drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Your original file is never changed — saving gives you a new copy, so if a cut comes out wrong just open it again and redraw.
