# AP Labeler: a Simulated name left behind is reported, and unnumbered APs are easy to spot in Manual order

Suite **2.246.13**, AP Labeler **2.22.0**.

## What changed

### AP Labeler (naming and download)

- **An AP that would still be named "Simulated …" is reported.** Ekahau gives a newly placed AP a name such as `Simulated AP-47`. When any AP on a floor being renamed would still carry one, an amber note under "Preview" gives the count and lists every such AP by floor. The floor name in the note opens that floor. The note updates as numbers are assigned and disappears when none are left.
- **"Download labeled .esx" asks first when any are left.** The question names each AP (up to twelve; past that, the count per floor, with every name listed under "Preview"). "OK" downloads the file as it is; "Cancel" returns to the plan without building anything. Floors excluded under "Floors to rename" and names the pattern writes itself, such as `AP-001`, are not reported.

### AP Labeler (Manual order)

- **APs still to be numbered are drawn as large white discs with a thick ring in the AP's own colour, edged dark inside and out.** The previous marker was a small disc with a thin dashed ring, which was easy to lose on a busy plan, and a pale colour such as yellow was hard to see on white paper. A numbered AP remains a smaller disc filled with its colour and showing its number, so the ringed discs are the ones left to do.
- **Clicking an AP that already has a number now says so.** A click on a numbered AP has always removed its number and moved the later ones down. A message now names the number removed and the AP, and says that "Undo" puts it back. Going over a floor a second time by clicking each AP removes each number in turn, so redoing a floor is done with "Clear floor".
- **The counter beside "Next" shows the floor on screen first.** It reads, for example, `2 of 19 on this floor · 22 of 39 numbered in all`. Before, it showed only the whole-project figure. On the "Unplaced" tab it shows the whole-project figure only.
- **User manual:** the "Manual (click order)" and "Download" sections describe the markers, the counter, and the Simulated report.

## Verified

- Tests drive the real handlers: the report lists exactly the Simulated names, per floor, redraws as numbers are assigned, opens the right floor from its button, and Download asks and builds nothing on "Cancel". Each of these fails when its behaviour is removed.
- A browser test in Chrome, Edge and Firefox measures the markers: an unnumbered AP is larger than a numbered one, its ring is at least 4 pixels, it is white inside and edged dark, and a yellow ring keeps those edges. It fails with the previous marker size.
- The full suite passes.
