# AP Labeler: manual numbering says when a click removed a number, and counts the floor on screen

Suite **2.246.11**, AP Labeler **2.20.1**.

## What changed

### AP Labeler (Manual order)

- **Clicking an AP that already has a number now says so.** With "Order" set to "Manual (click order)", a click on a numbered AP has always removed its number and moved the later ones down. A message now names the number removed and the AP it came from, and says that "Undo" puts it back. Going over a floor a second time by clicking each AP again removes each number in turn, so only the APs that were skipped stay numbered; the messages make that visible while it happens.
- **The counter beside "Next" shows the floor on screen first.** It reads, for example, `2 of 19 on this floor · 22 of 39 numbered in all`. Before, it showed only the whole-project figure, which was larger than the floor's own AP count. On the "Unplaced" tab it shows the whole-project figure only.
- **User manual:** the "Manual (click order)" section describes the counter and notes that redoing a floor by clicking through it again removes the numbers instead of replacing them.

## Verified

- Tests drive the real click handler and counter text: a click on a numbered AP still removes it and renumbers the rest, the message names the number and the AP, a first click shows no message, and the counter reports the floor's own tally and the project's. The new tests fail when the message or the floor tally is removed.
- The full suite passes.
