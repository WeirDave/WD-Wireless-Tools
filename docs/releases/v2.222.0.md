# Every report page can be turned; the printed reports state what they hold and fit the sheet

Suite **2.222.0**: Report **2.71.0**. Every other tool is unchanged.

## What changed

### Report: pages and printing

- **Every page of every report has the *Page* control**: **Auto**, **Portrait**, **Landscape**. Tables, the Summary, the BOM, the Coverage legend, the Design Review's findings and the Interference summary had none. Choosing an orientation changes that page only.
- **"Match all pages" now reaches every page**, the cover included. All portrait prints only portrait sheets and all landscape prints only landscape sheets, in Chromium (Chrome and Edge) and in Firefox.
- **On Auto**, a map turns landscape when it prints meaningfully larger that way, a table turns landscape when its columns will not fit across a portrait sheet, and any other page stays portrait. The cover on Auto follows the sheets that actually print; a single landscape AP Notes page no longer turns the cover of a BOM, Summary or Coverage report landscape in front of portrait sheets.
- **Plans taller than about 1.25 : 1 no longer run off the sheet** on Coverage, Interference, the RF Design Review channel maps and the Change / Audit overlay. Each floor is one fitted sheet, the same as the AP Placement Map, so no AP is cut off at the bottom and a 600 × 6000 plan no longer spreads over nine sheets. These sheets also gain the *Page* control.
- **A zoomed section prints on its floor's orientation in Firefox.** A sectioned floor set to landscape printed its first section landscape and every later one portrait.
- **No footer-only sheet at the end.** The closing credit line printed on a sheet of its own after a landscape AP Notes page; it now prints on the last page.
- **Reports print on white paper whatever the on-screen theme.** From the default dark theme, text such as the BOM's procurement notes printed in pale grey on white, and a PDF saved with background graphics had near-black margins. The page is the light theme while it prints; the saved theme choice is untouched.
- **Marker numbers stay at 6 pt or more** when a very tall plan is printed landscape.
- **Floors list in building, storey, then name order** in every report. A leading number in a floor name is read as a storey only when it is one or two digits ("200 Sample TF_Overall…" is no longer "Floor 200"), and a floor word after an underscore ("Block B_Level 02") is recognised.
- **A project with no access points** says so, instead of "Drop an .esx to render a report".
- **Template cards say when they cannot work.** A card for a report the open project cannot fill reads **Not for this project**, greyed, with the reason: **Interference / Rogue Devices** needs an Ekahau Survey walk; the plan-based reports need at least one AP.

### Report: AP Placement Map and Antenna Aim Sheet

- **AP numbers are legible on elongated plans** (more than about 2.8 : 1): the label pill grows with its text, and crowded labels search eight rings for room before an AP is cut back to its number. Labels avoid cones where they can.
- **An AP outside the plan is named** in a note under the map instead of being drawn on the corner.
- **A directional antenna with no azimuth reads "no azimuth"** on the Aim Sheet (outlined) instead of "omni", and a mixed-radio AP is aimed from its directional radio. An AP with no radio is not listed as directional.
- **The Aim Sheet's maps are fitted to the sheet** and use the Placement Map's markers, label placer and cone arrows. A 600 × 6000 corridor prints on five sheets instead of thirteen.
- **Azimuth prints as 0–359** (450° is 90°); **TX power on labels keeps its decimal** (14.5 dBm, not 15).
- **The key's colours match the markers**, and the reduced-label note names the fields that were dropped and where to find them.
- **The Summary strip sits on the cover**, no longer counts "no floor plan" as a floor plan, and no longer takes a sheet of its own.
- **Sectioned floors no longer print a footer-only sheet** per floor; the key-plan thumbnail's letters are 6.5 pt.
- **Opening *Configure grid…* and pressing Done without editing** no longer changes the report; **None** in the AP list respects an active search.

### Report: AP Installation, Summary and BOM

- **"Antennas in use" counts access points**, not radios, in the Installation legend and the Summary.
- **Disabled and non-Wi-Fi radios are left out** of the Installation table, the BOM antenna and mount counts and the Summary radio and band counts. The Installation channel column shows the width ("36/80").
- **Cover and overview agree on the floor count**, which excludes "(No floor plan)" and follows the AP filter.
- **Summary** — *Top AP models* is keyed by vendor and model with a **Vendor** column, an **Other (n models)** row past ten and a total row; the per-floor **Canvas** column is now **Size**, in the Settings unit.
- **BOM** — a **Not recorded** antenna row counts radios with no antenna, and the total reads **Total radios** when it appears.
- **Summary and BOM gain Client / company, Prepared by, Project reference and Revision** (the same four as the Placement Map, starting from Settings → Report), and the header carries them when the cover is off.
- **Summary and BOM print as a few shared sheets** instead of one section per sheet; a floor's table footer no longer lands on a sheet of its own; the floor summary matrix keeps its Total row with its table.
- Mount reads one way everywhere ("Ceiling", "Wall mount"), "5 GHz" replaces "FIVE", singular and plural are right on the cover, a negative height prints "—", and an unnamed AP's # cell is "—".

### Report: Interference / Rogue Devices

- **The device table has column widths.** The "Wide-channel Wi-Fi" chip is no longer clipped, a BSSID no longer wraps mid-address, and "Seen on" lists floors by name in floor order. The table is a page like the others: it turns landscape when **Channel(s)** is on and it will not fit.
- **AT&T hotspots and gateways are now recognised.** The pattern was never matched by a real name, so **Include AT&T (phone or gateway)** did nothing.
- **Floor maps** name the Medium and High devices and now say how many low-severity ones are left to the table; floors with no survey walk are not printed as "0 interferers detected"; a floor with no recorded walk path says so; the walk path is a readable width at any plan size; a design-only project's cover shows "—".
- A run of 20 MHz channels prints as a range (`ch1–61(6E)`), SSIDs sort naturally, and 6 GHz channel 2 is a 6 GHz channel.

### Report: Coverage Cell Boundary

- **Numbers that landed on each other are separated**, with a short line back to the AP.
- **Defaults are starred.** A radio with no stored power, antenna gain or channel prints `15 dBm*`, `3 dBi*`, `5 GHz*`, with a footnote; a floor plan with no scale draws no cell and says why.
- **Only APs that are drawn are numbered**, with no gaps; the rest are named under the table with the reason (*Not on the map*).
- **A multi-radio AP is drawn from its 5 GHz radio** and the Band column says "1 of 3 radios"; the key's ring wording and band swatches now match the map.

### Report: RF Design Review

- **A floor with no scale is a *Check* finding** that stays when notes are switched off, and its channel map says "Not checked" instead of "no co-channel radios". The result tiles count what is listed, mount heights print in the chosen unit, and the page states how many of the project's APs were reviewed.
- **The AP schedule export ignores an AP filter the open report hides**, and a disabled first radio no longer supplies an AP's height and mount.

### Report: Change / Audit Report

- **Channel, width, TX power and radio changes are compared** and listed (previously "Nothing changed … same hardware" while every radio had moved channel). Aim, tilt and height going from unset to set are listed, and added, removed and renamed floor plans have their own table.
- **Moves on a floor with no scale are measured in pixels** (10 px) and named; a plan re-imported at a different resolution is no longer reported as moved APs; a group of APs that moved together is called out; **Any distance at all** no longer reports every AP as moved.
- Tables share sheets, and the overlay's dots are sized for wide plans and carry the AP's name.

## Verified

- Every report was opened, with every option, over eleven invented projects (omni only, hostile names, a survey, a 600 × 6000 plan, an empty project, a 25-floor tower, CAD-style floor names) in Chromium and printed to PDF; sheet orientation, overlap, off-sheet text, blank sheets and text under 6 pt were measured on the PDFs.
- With **Match all pages** on Portrait and on Landscape, and with one page flipped, every sheet came out as asked in Chromium and in Firefox 157 through its own print pipeline.
- Each fix has a test that fails against the old code. The suite passes.
