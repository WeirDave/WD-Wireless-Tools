# Capacity and Quick Walls move into the workbench layout

Suite **2.200.0**, Capacity **1.6.0**, Quick Walls **7.70.0**.

## What changed

### Capacity

- **Capacity now uses the workbench layout shared with Prep, PlanTrim and AP Labeler.** Its three steps are listed down a rail on the left: **1 · What this project asks for**, **2 · Save as a template** and **3 · Apply a template**. Clicking one jumps to it. The steps themselves fill the middle column, which scrolls on its own.
- **"Apply and download" has moved to a footer along the bottom** that never scrolls out of view, beside the line that says what it will write ("Pick a template first." until a template is chosen). It was at the foot of the third step, below the per-floor list, so on a project with many floors it sat far down the page.
- Step 2 on the rail says that it opens once the project has requirement areas to read, which is when its card appears.
- The note that the project file is never written to now sits at the foot of the rail.

### Quick Walls

- **Quick Walls now uses the workbench layout shared with Prep, PlanTrim, AP Labeler and Capacity.** The **Keyboard shortcuts** 1–9 run down a rail on the left, with the tips on assigning them beneath. The project's wall types fill the middle column, which scrolls on its own. The **Template** controls sit in a panel on the right.
- **The Template panel** holds what was a single bar across the top of the page: the **Saved templates** picker, **Apply**, **Save Template**, **Manage**, **Ekahau Defaults** and **Auto-apply on open**, with a line on what applying a template does. Every control works as before.
- **"Save the *.esx" has moved to a footer along the bottom** that never scrolls out of view. Beside it, the footer says what the save will write, for example "Saves Project_modified.esx with 21 wall types and 9 shortcuts. Your file is not changed." It counts only the shortcuts 1–9 that the save keeps.
- **Wall type cards no longer break their names across lines.** The list sizes its columns from the space it has, and each card puts its **Set key**, **Edit**, **Clone** and **Delete** buttons under the name instead of beside it. Long names such as "Door, Interior Office" read on one line.
- **Visual Swap** is unchanged.

## Verified

- Capacity was opened in headless Chromium against the real server with an invented two-floor project carrying requirement areas, at 1600×900 and 1280×720, in the dark and light themes. All three steps render, "Apply and download" stays on screen at both sizes, and there were no console errors. Clicking **3 · Apply a template** on the rail scrolled the steps column to it.
- The page is parsed to check that the rail lists the three steps, every step points at a card that exists in the steps column, and the button and its note are in the footer outside the scrolling column. Pointing a step at a missing card, or moving the footer into the column, fails the test.
- Quick Walls was opened in headless Chromium against the real server with an invented project, at 1600×900, 1500×894 and 1280×720, in the dark and light themes. The shortcuts, wall types and template panel render; "Save the *.esx" stays on screen; every wall type name reads in full; Visual Swap opens; there were no console errors.
- The page is parsed to check that the shortcuts are on the rail, the wall types in the middle, the template controls in the panel, and the save button in the footer outside the scrolling columns.
- The footer sentence runs in Node from the page's own code: it names the file the save writes, counts the wall types and only the 1–9 shortcuts, and promises a copy. Counting a shortcut outside 1–9 fails the test.
- The full test suite passes.
