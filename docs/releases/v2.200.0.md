# Capacity moves into the workbench layout

Suite **2.200.0**, Capacity **1.6.0**.

## What changed

### Capacity

- **Capacity now uses the workbench layout shared with Prep, PlanTrim and AP Labeler.** Its three steps are listed down a rail on the left: **1 · What this project asks for**, **2 · Save as a template** and **3 · Apply a template**. Clicking one jumps to it. The steps themselves fill the middle column, which scrolls on its own.
- **"Apply and download" has moved to a footer along the bottom** that never scrolls out of view, beside the line that says what it will write ("Pick a template first." until a template is chosen). It was at the foot of the third step, below the per-floor list, so on a project with many floors it sat far down the page.
- Step 2 on the rail says that it opens once the project has requirement areas to read, which is when its card appears.
- The note that the project file is never written to now sits at the foot of the rail.

## Verified

- Capacity was opened in headless Chromium against the real server with an invented two-floor project carrying requirement areas, at 1600×900 and 1280×720, in the dark and light themes. All three steps render, "Apply and download" stays on screen at both sizes, and there were no console errors. Clicking **3 · Apply a template** on the rail scrolled the steps column to it.
- The page is parsed to check that the rail lists the three steps, every step points at a card that exists in the steps column, and the button and its note are in the footer outside the scrolling column. Pointing a step at a missing card, or moving the footer into the column, fails the test.
- The full test suite passes.
