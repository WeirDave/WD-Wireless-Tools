# Settings: a card of its own for every section, and a calmer way to switch between them

Suite **2.246.2**. Tool versions are unchanged.

## What changed

### Settings (Menu → Suite Settings)

- **Every section has its own icon.** General, Subfolder Structure and Backup & restore carried the suite logo, so three of the nine cards looked the same. They now have a sliders icon, a folder icon and a box-with-arrow icon, in a neutral grey that reads on both themes.
- **Choosing a card no longer moves the page or flashes the section.** The section appears where the cards are and the page stays put. Before, the page scrolled so the section sat at the top of the window, which on a narrow window carried the cards out of view. Arriving from a tool's Settings link still scrolls to and marks the section, as before.
- **The address still names the open section**, so a reload or a copied link opens the same one.
- **Backup & restore reads as separate points.** The export paragraph is three short paragraphs instead of one block broken by line breaks.
- **The "What is saved" lines sit closer to their "Changed in …" note**, so each setting reads as one entry.

## Verified

- The Settings page was checked in the browser's built-in Chromium engine, in dark and light, at 1280px, 900px and 540px wide. The Firefox, Chrome and Edge CI jobs run on the push.
- New tests cover choosing a card (the right section opens, the page does not scroll, the address updates, the card is marked) and one distinct icon for each section.
- The full suite passes.
