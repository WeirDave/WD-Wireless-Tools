# About window: wide enough to read the release notes of an available update

Suite **2.235.4**. Every tool is unchanged.

## What changed

### About window (**About** in any tool's menu)

- **The window is wide and in two columns.** It was a 480 px strip. It is now up to 1100 px wide (94% of the window on a narrower screen). On the left: the version installed, **Diagnostics** with the log file location, **Links** and the credit line. On the right: **Check for updates**, its result and, when a newer release exists, the update panel.
- **The release notes of an available update are easier to read.** The list headed **What's new since your version** was capped at 220 px, and shorter on a short screen. It is now as tall as the screen allows, up to 420 px, and about 590 px wide, so a release's bullets read as single lines. The first release in the list can be opened with its arrow, as before.
- **No scrollbar on the window itself at laptop size.** The list takes what is left of the screen height; on a 1366 × 768 screen with a browser's own toolbars the whole window fits without scrolling.
- **Narrow windows keep the old single column.** Below 760 px wide the update comes first, then Diagnostics and Links, in the original order.

## Verified

- The About window is opened with an update available (five invented releases with long notes) at 1366 × 768 in Chrome, Edge and Firefox: the window is at least 1000 px wide, the list is at least 500 px wide and 220 px tall, the window does not scroll, the update sits to the right of Diagnostics, and Diagnostics and Links stay together instead of being spread to the height of the notes. At 700 px wide the update appears above Diagnostics. With the stylesheet change removed, the test fails in all three browsers.
