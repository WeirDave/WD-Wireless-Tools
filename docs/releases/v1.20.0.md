Report gets a **Site Summary Sheet** template — a client-facing one-pager pulling APs, floors, buildings, radios, top models, and antennas together in one place. First of five new report types tracked for this cycle.

Suite `v1.19.0 → v1.20.0`. Report `v2.6 → v2.7`.

## What's new

New template in the Report gallery: **Site Summary Sheet** (teal cover accent, distinct from AP Placement's blue and Phone Hotspots' navy). Uses the whole project regardless of the AP filter — this is meta content, not a per-AP walkthrough.

### Sections rendered

1. **Cover page** — site name, total AP count, floor count, logo, date.
2. **Project at a glance** — variable-count stat tiles: APs · Radios · Floor plans · Buildings · Antenna types · Surveys · Measured radios. Zero-count categories are hidden.
3. **Per-floor breakdown** — table showing every floor plan with its AP count, associated building, and canvas dimensions. Unplaced APs get their own row.
4. **Radio band breakdown** — horizontal bar chart of radios by band (2.4 / 5 / 6 GHz / Unspecified). Percentages tabular-aligned. Uses radio-level counting, not AP-level — one AP with 2.4+5+6 GHz radios contributes 3 rows.
5. **Top AP models** — up to 10 most-used models with quantities.
6. **Antennas in use** — every antenna model referenced with band, coupling, gain, and beam width.

### Sidebar options

Four toggles (all default on):
- Per-floor breakdown table
- Radio band breakdown
- Top AP models
- Antennas in use

Users who want a lean summary can turn off any section without losing the cover + stats.
