*"Then let's just call the wall 'Framery Boxes'."* — Dave.

The wall type shipped in [v1.31.0](v1.31.0.md) as *Framery One* — named after the single-person pod. But Framery makes a whole lineup (Compact, One, 2Q, Four, Six), and their construction is close enough — steel frame, acoustic wool sandwich, laminated glass door — that a single wall type covers all of them for predictive planning. No point making the reader hunt for a "Framery Four" type that doesn't exist and squinting at whether *One* is close enough.

**Fix.** Renamed the wall type to **Framery Boxes** — one bucket, all models. Whether the pod on the floor plan is a Compact, a 2Q, or a Six, the same wall type gets drawn around its footprint.

Kept everything else steady:

- Attenuation: `300` across 2.4 / 5 / 6 GHz (~18 dB effective at 0.06 m thickness)
- Reflection: `0.405`
- Diffraction: `11`
- Thickness: `0.06 m`
- Color: `#9C27B0`

Anyone whose local Ekahau project still has walls typed as "Framery One" won't lose them — they'll show as an unrecognized custom type in Quick Walls and can be swapped to "Framery Boxes" via Visual Wall Swap in one pass.
