# Report adds an RF Design Review and an AP schedule spreadsheet

Suite **2.213.0**: Report **2.70.0**. Every other tool is unchanged.

## What changed

### Report

- **New template: "RF Design Review".** A pre-flight check of a design before it goes to a client or an installer. In **Report**, pick **RF Design Review** under *Audit & Change*, then **Generate report**. It opens with a count of items to **Fix**, to **Check** and to **Note**, and one line saying whether the design is ready to hand over. Each finding lists the access points it applies to, with the floor and the detail.
- **What it checks:**
  - co-channel neighbours: two radios on the same floor whose channels overlap, closer than **"Co-channel neighbours closer than"** (default 20 m; 10, 15 and 30 m are offered). Overlap is by 20 MHz channel, so a 36/80 and a 44/20 count. Each radio is listed with its nearest co-channel neighbour and the distance;
  - 2.4 GHz radios off the **"2.4 GHz channel plan"** (default 1 / 6 / 11; 1 / 5 / 9 / 13 is offered) or wider than 20 MHz, and 5 GHz radios at 160 MHz;
  - 2.4 GHz radios less than 3 dB below the 5 GHz radio on the same AP, with **"Check 2.4 GHz runs at least 3 dB below 5 GHz"** (on by default);
  - radios above **"Flag transmit power above"** (default 20 dBm; 14, 17 and 23 dBm, or no check, are offered), and radios with no channel or no transmit power;
  - directional antennas with no azimuth, access points sharing a name, Ekahau's default names and MAC addresses as names, no model, no mount height, and access points on no floor plan;
  - as notes: DFS channels, 6 GHz channels with no preferred scanning channel, and floors with no scale, where distances are not measured. **"Include notes"** turns these off.
- **Channel plan table.** Per floor and band, how many radios use each channel and width (**"Channel plan table"**, on by default).
- **Channel map per floor.** One band on the plan (**"Band on the channel map"**, default 5 GHz), every radio labelled with its channel and coloured by it, and a dashed red line between co-channel radios closer than the chosen distance (**"Channel map per floor"**, on by default).
- Disabled radios and radios that are not Wi-Fi are left out of every check. The findings run on from one to the next in print; each channel map starts its own sheet.
- **"AP schedule (CSV)".** A new button on the review step of every report, beside **Print / Save PDF**. It saves one row per access point ticked in the AP filter: name, floor, building, vendor, model, mount, height, azimuth, tilt, antenna, then channel, width and transmit power for 2.4, 5 and 6 GHz, the column grid reference, the position on the plan, and the AP's notes. Lengths follow the unit chosen in Settings and the header names it. The file opens in Excel with every character intact, and a cell that begins like a spreadsheet formula is written so that it is not run as one.

## Verified

- Each rule fires on a synthetic project built to trigger it, and does not fire on its counterpart: a disabled radio and a Bluetooth radio half a metre from another radio on the same channel, a 6 GHz radio on a preferred scanning channel, and a 2.4 GHz radio exactly 3 dB below 5 GHz. Removing any one rule fails a test.
- The report was opened, generated and printed in Firefox and Chromium from a synthetic two-floor, 72-AP design. Both printed 9 sheets, down from 21 when every finding started a sheet. No text overlaps, none is closer than 18 pt to a sheet edge, and the smallest is 6.75 pt.
- The CSV button was clicked in Firefox and Chromium. Chromium saved `Report - AP Schedule - <project>.csv` with one row per access point.
- The full test suite passes.
