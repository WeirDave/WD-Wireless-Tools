# Prep: the trim is drawn on the floor plan before anything is written

Suite **2.178.0** ships **Prep 1.11.0**. The Prep page shows the Prep number, not the suite number. This release also carries Cloud Manager 4.72.1 from suite 2.177.1.

## What changed

### Prep: a map of what the trim cuts

- **Prep now shows the floor plan with the crop drawn on it**, the same view PlanTrim uses. The whole sheet is shown, the paper that will be cut away is shaded, and a dashed blue line marks what is kept.
- **Where it is:** at the top of step **2 — What that would do**, above the step cards. It appears whenever **Trim the canvas** is ticked in step 1, and is on by default because that box is ticked by default.
- **Choosing a floor:** a row per floor sits above the plan, each reading **Trim**, **Leave as is** or **Refused** with the size change or the reason. Click a row to see that floor. The map opens on the first floor that is actually being cropped. A floor that is not cropped is shown without shading, with the reason underneath.
- **It follows the options.** The map redraws whenever the preview does, so changing **Leave a margin of** moves the dashed line, and ticking **Use the rectangles I drew in PlanTrim** shows those rectangles instead of the automatic crop.
- It works both for a dropped project and for one opened with **Open from disk…**. Opened from disk, the floor images are read from the project where it sits, one floor at a time; nothing is uploaded.
- The map is a view only. Drawing a rectangle by hand is still done in PlanTrim.

## Verified

- Driven in Chromium against a synthetic project, both dropped and opened from disk: the kept region is drawn unshaded, the cut paper is shaded, changing the margin to Wide widens the kept region, and the page reported no errors.
- The kept rectangle is placed from the same report the Trim card quotes, and scales correctly onto a plan rendered at a different size from its coordinate space (vector plans).
- The floor rows select a floor through the page's own control dispatcher.
- The floor image route returns only a plan image listed in the project's floor plans, is served sandboxed, and refuses anything else.
- Firefox and Edge were not available in the environment this was built in and have not been checked.
