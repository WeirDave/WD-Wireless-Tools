# PlanTrim moves into the workbench layout; Prep drops Wall swap

Suite **2.196.0**, PlanTrim **2.0.0**, Prep **2.1.0**.

## What changed

### PlanTrim

- **PlanTrim now uses the same workbench layout as Prep.** The floors run down a rail on the left, the plan fills the middle, this floor's controls sit in a panel on the right, and **Cut and save** is in a footer along the bottom that never scrolls out of view. The footer also says what the save will write, for example "2 floor plans will be cropped (1 to a box you drew)".
- Each floor is a card on the rail with its name, its state (**Automatic**, **Your box**, **Nothing to do** or **Cannot crop**) and its numbers. A refusal reason is wrapped and readable in full rather than clipped. **Next floor →** sits under the cards.
- The right-hand panel holds **✂ Crop to this box** and **Edit box**, **Margin around the drawing**, **Suggest from the set**, **Apply to all floors** and **Back to automatic**. **↺ Reset view** sits above the plan.
- The duplicate **Save trimmed .esx** button in the header is gone; **Cut and save** in the footer is the one place to save.
- **Fixed: a crop made while PlanTrim was still reading the project is no longer lost.** Pressing **Crop to this box** during the first few seconds after opening a project cropped and saved the box, but the floor went on reading "Automatic · your box was not used" and the footer "cropped automatically" until something else triggered a new reading. The new reading is now queued and runs as soon as the first one finishes.
- Everything else in PlanTrim behaves as before, and its controls keep their names.

### Prep

- **Prep's rail has three stages: 1 · Trim the canvas, 2 · Requirement areas and 3 · Wall types.** The fourth, **Wall swap**, is gone from the rail, the settings panel and the server's project facts.
- Prep gets a new project's `.esx` ready to work in. Changing the type of walls already drawn, after an import has put concrete and steel on the same type, is a later job and stays in Quick Walls' **Wall Swap**.

## Verified

- PlanTrim was driven in headless Chromium against the real server, on a synthetic two-floor project, at 1600×900 and 1280×720, in the dark and light themes. A rectangle was drawn by dragging on the plan and cropped. **Crop to this box** became the one primary control, the floor read **Your box** and the footer counted the drawn box. **Cut and save** stayed on screen at both sizes, and there were no console errors.
- The queued reading runs on the real `__ptAnalyze` and `analyze` against a stub server: a second request waits for the first, then goes out carrying the cropped box, and the floors end on that reading. Removing the queue fails the test.
- PlanTrim's markup is parsed to check that the action footer is a direct child of the workbench, beside the scrolling columns and not inside one. Moving it into a column fails the test.
- Prep's markup is read for the stages its rail opens: trim, areas and walls, in that order, each with its own panel and no other. No text on the page offers Wall Swap. The test fails on the 2.195.0 page.
- The full test suite passes.
