# Readable workbench tools; AP Labeler and PlanTrim laid out around the job

Suite **2.202.0**: Quick Walls **7.71.0**, PlanTrim **2.1.0**, AP Labeler **2.16.0**, Capacity **1.7.0**, Prep **2.2.0**, Settings.

## What changed

### Quick Walls, PlanTrim, AP Labeler, Capacity and Prep

- **No text that is meant to be read is smaller than 14 pixels, and nothing on these pages is smaller than 13.** Explanations, hints and notes were set at 11–12 pixels and badges at 9–10, which at a browser zoom of 90% reached the screen at about 10 pixels. Explanations, hints, the left-hand panel's notes and table rows are now 14; labels, controls and the sentence beside each tool's main button are 15; the names of steps, wall types and templates are 16; uppercase badges and column headings are 13.
- The page header that every tool shares is unchanged.
- The same sizes apply to text that only appears in some states: empty lists, warnings, dialogs, the colour sequence, the wall audit, and a project still being read.

- **The open file's name no longer runs under the tool's title.** On a window half a monitor wide, a long project name in the header ran underneath the centred title. The name now stops short of the title and ends in "…", and pointing at it shows the whole name.

### PlanTrim

- **The panel says what PlanTrim is for.** It opens with **Keep the building, lose the rest**: a sheet from the architect carries a title block, borders and notes, the parking lot and white space, and PlanTrim cuts them away so the floor plan is centred and easy to work on. Below it, the two ways to do that - **Let PlanTrim find the building** (automatic, the dashed outline on the plan) or **Or draw it yourself** (drag a box) - replace "With no rectangle, the drawing is found automatically".
- **Check the corners before you cut.** A new **Check the corners before you cut** box has four buttons - **Top left**, **Top right**, **Bottom left**, **Bottom right** - that each zoom right in on one corner of what will be kept, so a faint or thin line at the edge can be checked before saving. **Reset view** goes back to the whole plan.
- **Floors read in words.** A floor's card in the **Floors** list says how much of the sheet is cut away ("cuts away 64% of the sheet") instead of pixel sizes, which are kept on the card's tooltip. The line beside the plan says "Your box cuts away 64% of the sheet" and no longer shows a negative percentage when a box reaches past the sheet's edge. The repeated "Drag a rectangle over the part of the sheet to keep" under the panel is gone.
- **A tall building fits the Floors list.** Past eight floors, each card shows the floor's name and its state on one line, the detail moves to the tooltip, and the list scrolls inside the rail with the floor being worked on kept in view. The note under the list stays in place.
- The margin control is now labelled **Space to leave around the building**.

### Settings

- **Each tool's Settings link opens that tool's settings.** **Suite Settings** in the menu of PlanTrim, AP Labeler, Capacity and Prep now opens Settings at the section holding that tool's settings, with the other sections folded and the section marked for a moment. PlanTrim and AP Labeler open **PlanTrim, Rename and the rest**; Capacity and Prep open **Capacity**. The **Settings → Capacity** links inside Capacity and Prep used to land at the top of the page; they open the Capacity section now.

### AP Labeler

- **What the names will look like sits directly under the name pattern.** The preview used to be docked at the bottom of the right-hand panel and listed every access point on the floor. It is now the section right after **Name Pattern**: a one-line summary, the common part of the old and new names, and three example rows - the first two names on the floor and the last - each shown old → new.
- **Every name is one press away, in its own window.** **Show all N names** under the examples opens the complete list for the floor, old and new, in a large window that scrolls. **Close**, Escape or a click outside it closes it.
- **A building with dozens of floors fits the Floors list.** Past eight floors, the **Floors** list on the left becomes a grid of compact buttons - the floor's name and its access-point count on one line - that scrolls on its own, so the note under it stays in place. Choosing a floor keeps its button in view. Sixty floors take two columns and a short scroll instead of three screens.
- **"Choose floors" no longer pushes the rest of the panel away.** Under **Floors to rename → Choose floors**, the floor checkboxes are laid out in columns in a box of their own that scrolls, at most about a third of the window tall. **Tick all** and **Tick none** stay above it.
- The floor counts on the **Floors** buttons are drawn at the new text size rather than a fixed 11 pixels.

### Capacity

- The cards are wider on a large monitor: up to 1280 pixels instead of 1080.

### Cloud Manager

- A browser-cookie reader that fails to load no longer stops the server starting. On Windows that reader starts a component whose cache file two programs starting together can read half-written; the failure is now written to the log and the reader is tried again at the next Cloud Manager sign-in.

## Verified

- In headless Firefox at 2519×1279, each of the five tools was opened with an invented project and every visible piece of text outside the shared header was measured: none is below 13 pixels. The same check on the previous release finds text below 13 pixels in all five.
- Each tool was opened and looked at in headless Firefox at 2519×1279 and 1366×1080.
- In headless Firefox, Capacity, PlanTrim, Quick Walls and AP Labeler were each opened with an invented project whose name is about ninety characters long, at 1280 and 1100 pixels wide: the file name ends before the title starts. Without the change it ran under the title in Capacity, PlanTrim and Quick Walls.
- In headless Firefox with an invented sixty-floor project: the floor list scrolls inside the rail with the rail's note on screen; choosing floor 47 leaves its button in view; the example section comes directly after **Name Pattern** and shows at most three names; **Show all 6 names** opens a window listing all six and Escape closes it; and **Choose floors** lists all sixty in a box no taller than 40% of the window.
- In headless Firefox, every link into Settings from any tool was followed: each opened a section and brought it to the top of the window, or as high as the end of the page allows, and each tool's link opened that tool's section.
- In headless Firefox with an invented 43-floor project at 1366×900: the Floors list scrolls inside the rail with its note on screen; each corner button zooms to at least three times the fitted scale with that corner of what will be kept at the centre of the plan; and the drawing instruction appears once. All three failed on the previous code.
- The full test suite passes.
