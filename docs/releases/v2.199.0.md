# AP Labeler moves into the workbench layout, in its own purple throughout

Suite **2.199.0**, AP Labeler **2.15.0**.

## What changed

### AP Labeler

- **AP Labeler now uses the workbench layout shared with Prep and PlanTrim.** The floors run down a rail on the left as cards, each with its access-point count, the plan fills the middle, and the naming panel stays on the right, still resizable by dragging its edge.
- **"Download labeled .esx" has moved from the header to a footer along the bottom** that never scrolls out of view. Beside it, the footer says what the download will do, for example "20 of 20 access points will be renamed. A new copy is downloaded; your file is not changed." It names how many new names would be used more than once, and says "Nothing to rename" when every access point already has the name the pattern gives it.
- A floor left out of **Floors to rename** is dimmed on the rail, as it was in the tabs.
- **AP Labeler is purple throughout**: the active pattern tab, the new names in the preview, the numbering controls, the add-segment button and the panel's resize handle. They were indigo, a different colour from the tool's header and Home tile.

### PlanTrim

- The user guide no longer mentions a save button in the top right of the page, which was removed in suite 2.196.0.

## Verified

- AP Labeler was opened in headless Chromium against the real server with an invented two-floor project of twenty access points, at 1600×900, 1400×800 and 1280×720, in the dark and light themes. The floors are on the rail, "Download labeled .esx" stays on screen at every size, the footer reads "20 of 20 access points will be renamed", and there were no console errors.
- The footer sentence runs in Node from the page's own code: it counts only the access points whose name changes, says when nothing would, names duplicates, and promises only a new copy. Counting every access point instead fails the test.
- The page is parsed to check that the download is in the footer, the footer sits beside the workbench and not inside a scrolling column, and the floors are on the rail.
- The full test suite passes.
