# Settings: the panel no longer has empty margins either side

Suite **2.246.6**. Tool versions are unchanged.

## What changed

### Settings (the panel over Home or over any tool)

- **The content fills the panel from edge to edge.** In 2.246.5 the panel was 1480px wide while the content inside it stopped at 1200px, so on a wide screen there was an empty band of about 150px on the left and on the right. The panel is now 1280px wide and the content fills it, so nothing is left over.
- **The Settings page on its own is capped at the same 1280px**, so it and the panel match. The panel is still wider than the 860px it was before 2.246.5.

## Verified

- The panel was opened from Home in the browser's built-in Chromium engine at a 1900px-wide window: the cards start at the panel's left edge and the settings box ends at its right edge.
- A new browser test, run in Firefox, Chrome and Edge in CI, opens the panel at a wide window size and fails if the cards, the title or the settings box sit more than 48px in from either edge. Before the change it measured 152 to 167px on both sides in all three browsers.
- The full suite passes.
