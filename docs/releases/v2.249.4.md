# The plan in Prep and Capacity no longer jumps when a crop box is drawn

Suite **2.249.4**, Prep **2.11.2**, Capacity **1.15.1**. The other tools are unchanged.

## What changed

### Prep

- **Drawing a crop box on the Trim stage no longer makes the plan jump up and down.** Each time a box was let go, the sheet was stretched about 10% taller until the project had been re-read, then snapped back, and the box being drawn drifted away from the pointer in the meantime. The plan now stays where it is.
- **Dragging a box is smoother.** The sheet is redrawn at most once per screen refresh instead of once per mouse movement, so a drag on a large plan no longer queues up redraws.
- **The footer keeps its height while the project is re-read** after a box is let go. The line starting "Will write:" is emptied during that moment, which shrank the footer and moved the plan's whole stage by two lines, then moved it back.
- **The sentence under the plan holds two lines** ("Drag a handle or an edge to adjust your box…"), so the plan is not resized when the sentence changes between one line and two.

### Capacity

- **The plan on the area step follows its frame in the same way.** It keeps its place and scale when the space around it changes height, and a drawn area stays under the pointer.

### Plan view (shared by Prep and Capacity)

- **The canvas resizes with its frame, not only with the window.** A small change in the frame's height keeps the plan centred where it was; a change of more than 15% in either direction refits a view that has not been moved, as a window resize always did.
- **Pointer positions are read against the canvas's own pixels**, so a box and its pointer agree even for the moment the frame is changing size.
- **Panning** is redrawn at most once per screen refresh as well.

## Verified

- The full suite passes. New tests drive the real plan view against a stage that changes height and check the bitmap, the framing, the pointer position and the redraw count, and drive the real preview code to check that the footer keeps its height until the answer is drawn. Each of them fails when its fix is taken out.
- A real drag in Chrome on a synthetic project, at 100% and 150% display scaling: the plan's frame measured 300px before, during and after the box was let go; before the change it measured 331px for the length of the re-read with the picture stretched to fit.
