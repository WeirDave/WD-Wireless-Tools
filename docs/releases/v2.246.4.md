# Settings: the open section's card is now easy to pick out

Suite **2.246.4**. Tool versions are unchanged.

## What changed

### Settings (Menu → Suite Settings, and the Settings panel)

- **The card for the section that is open is unmistakable.** It has a strong tint of its tool's colour, a second outline just outside the first, and a small arrow at its right edge pointing at the section. Before, it differed from the other eight cards only by a faint tint and a thin inner line, so it was hard to tell which section was showing.
- **The open card's name is in the page's normal text colour.** On the stronger tint the lighter tool colour fell below readable contrast in the light theme. The other eight cards keep their tool-coloured names.

## Verified

- The page was checked in the browser's built-in Chromium engine: Cloud Manager's card in the dark theme and Squirrel's in the light theme. The Firefox, Chrome and Edge CI jobs run on the push.
- Tests cover the open card's tint strength, its name colour, and the name's contrast against the tinted card for every section in both themes.
- The full suite passes.
