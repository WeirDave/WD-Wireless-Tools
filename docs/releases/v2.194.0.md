# Report: a "Back to top" button on the Review step

Suite **2.194.0**, Report **2.67.0**.

## What changed

### Report

- **A floating "↑ Back to top" button now appears in the bottom-right corner of the Review & Print step** once the bar holding **Print / Save PDF** has scrolled off-screen. Clicking it scrolls back up to that bar, so printing no longer means scrolling back through every sheet of a long report.
- It is on by default, has no setting, and appears only on the Review & Print step.
- It never prints: it is excluded from the printed document like the rest of the page controls.

## Verified

- The real stage switch, scroll handler and click handler run in Node against stubs: the button stays hidden at the top of the review, appears once scrolled past the bar, hides again on the way back up, stays hidden on the other steps, and its own handler scrolls the window to the top.
- Rendered in Chromium in light and dark themes.
- The full test suite passes.
