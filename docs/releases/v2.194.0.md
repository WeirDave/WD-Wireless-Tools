# Report: floating "Print / Save PDF" and "Back to top" buttons on the Review step

Suite **2.194.0**, Report **2.67.0**.

## What changed

### Report

- **Two floating buttons, "↑ Back to top" and "Print / Save PDF", now appear in the bottom-right corner of the Review & Print step** once the bar at the top of the report has scrolled off-screen. Printing from the bottom of a long report no longer means scrolling back through every sheet.
- The floating **Print / Save PDF** does exactly what the one in the top bar does. **Back to top** scrolls up to that bar, where **Back to configure** and **Open another…** also are.
- Both are on by default, have no setting, appear only on the Review & Print step, and never print: they are excluded from the printed document like the rest of the page controls.

## Verified

- The real stage switch, scroll handler and Back to top handler run in Node against stubs: the pair stays hidden at the top of the review, appears once scrolled past the bar, hides again on the way back up, stays hidden on the other steps, and Back to top scrolls the window to the top.
- The floating Print is checked against the top bar's Print: same action and handler. Pointing it anywhere else fails the test.
- Rendered in Chromium in light and dark themes.
- The full test suite passes.
