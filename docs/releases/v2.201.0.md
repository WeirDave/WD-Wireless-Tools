# Scale puts the converter at the top of the page

Suite **2.201.0**, Scale **1.6.0**.

## What changed

### Scale

- **The imperial and metric inputs now sit just under the header.** The page used to open on a large logo with a centred title and two lines of introduction, which put both inputs half way down the window. The title is now one compact row beside a small logo, with the same introduction and paste examples beside it.
- The converter, its outputs, the **Copy** buttons and the **Accepted formats** list work exactly as before. At 1400×900 the whole tool, including the formats list, fits without scrolling.
- Scale keeps its own layout rather than the workbench used by the file tools: it has no project to open and nothing to save, so a floor rail and a save footer would have nothing to hold.

## Verified

- Scale was opened in headless Chromium against the real server at 1400×900, 1280×720 and 760×900, in the dark and light themes. A measurement typed into the imperial input converted on both sides, the converter's top edge moved from 435px to 188px at 1400×900, the narrow window stacks the two sides, and there were no console errors.
- A check reads the rules the browser applies to the page and fails if the logo grows back above 80px or returns to sitting above the title. Restoring the old 180px logo fails it.
- The full test suite passes.

## Files changed

- `web/assets/wd-tools.css`: Scale's title row and page spacing
- `tests/test_scale_puts_the_converter_first.py`: new
