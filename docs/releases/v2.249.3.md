# Prep's requirement area no longer looks like it stays a rectangle while a shape is drawn

Suite **2.249.3**, Prep **2.11.1**. The other tools are unchanged.

## What changed

### Prep

- **While a requirement area is being drawn, the automatic area is shown faintly.** On the **Requirement areas** stage, choosing **Draw a shape** or **Draw a rectangle** used to leave the automatic area (the rectangle taken from the trimmed canvas) painted at full strength, so the plan still showed a rectangle and its label sat where the first corner is clicked. The automatic area is now a faint dashed outline, marking what the new area will replace, and its label is hidden until the drawing is finished or cancelled.
- **Nothing changes in what is written.** A finished shape was already the outline that Prepare writes; the plan now shows only that shape once it is closed.
- **To finish a shape**: click each corner, then click the first corner, double-click, or press Enter. **Undo last corner**, Backspace or Ctrl+Z takes the last corner back, and Esc cancels.

## Verified

- The full suite passes. A browser test in Chrome, Edge and Firefox opens Prep with Trim ticked, reads the painted plan at a point inside the automatic area, draws a triangle with **Draw a shape**, and checks that the point is solid before drawing, faint while drawing, and empty once the triangle exists, and that the triangle's corners are what Prepare is sent. It fails without the change.
