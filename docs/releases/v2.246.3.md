# Settings looks the same everywhere: cards on the left, settings and buttons on the right

Suite **2.246.3**. Tool versions are unchanged.

## What changed

### Settings (the panel over Home or over any tool, and the page at Menu → Suite Settings)

- **The cards sit down the left and the open section, with Save and Cancel, sits to the right, at every window width.** Below about 1000px the page used to move the cards into a block across the top, and the panel (860px wide) always did. The layout now narrows instead: the cards lose their description line and then shrink, and the section keeps its column beside them. Only on a phone-sized window, 560px or narrower, do the cards stack above the section as a grid.
- **The panel is wider, up to 1240px**, so on an ordinary screen it shows the same layout and proportions as the page does on its own. The User Guide panel is the same size.
- **Save and Cancel sit at the bottom of the panel and the page.** On a short section they used to float part-way down, with a large empty area beneath them. The page now fills the window or panel, and the buttons stay at the bottom edge.
- **The panel no longer shows a scrollbar when nothing needs scrolling.** The extra space at the bottom of the page inside the panel is gone.
- **The card list no longer grows a scrollbar of its own in a short window.** When the window is narrow and short, the cards drop their description line so all nine fit.

## Verified

- The panel was opened from Home in the browser's built-in Chromium engine, at 1500px and 900px wide windows: the cards sit down the left, the section is beside them, the buttons are flush with the bottom, and neither the page nor the card list scrolls. The Firefox, Chrome and Edge CI jobs run on the push.
- New tests cover the panel being wide enough for the side-by-side layout, the cards stacking only on a phone-sized window, and the buttons staying at the bottom of the page.
- The full suite passes.
