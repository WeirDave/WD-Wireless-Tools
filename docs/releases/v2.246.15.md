# Capacity: the "Draw the area" plan fills the window

Suite **2.246.15**, Capacity **1.14.0**.

## What changed

### Capacity (step 2, "Draw the area")

- **The plan now uses the whole width of the window and most of its height.** It was a box limited to 520 pixels tall and 1280 pixels wide, so on a large screen a floor plan was shrunk to fit the height and sat in the middle with empty black bands on both sides. The plan now takes the full width of the page and the height left under the floor buttons, so the area can be drawn at a usable size.
- **Smaller windows are unchanged.** The plan is never shorter than before (480 pixels), and the other steps ("Template", "Apply it to this project", "What this project already has") keep their previous width.
- Fit, zoom and panning work as before; "Fit" re-centres the sheet in the larger plan.

## Verified

- A test opens a project in Firefox, Chrome and Edge at a large window size and measures the plan: it is wider than the old 1280-pixel limit, fills its card, takes most of the window height, and the page does not scroll sideways.
- The full suite passes.
