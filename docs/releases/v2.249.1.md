# Cloud Manager marks the newer copy with a Newer tag, and the two replace buttons now match

Suite **2.249.1**, Cloud Manager **4.97.0**. The other tools are unchanged.

## What changed

### Cloud Manager

- **The copy that was saved most recently carries a green Newer tag beside its date.** It appears on the cloud side or the local side of a row, whichever was saved last, and disappears once a comparison shows the two are identical.
- **The sentences "The cloud copy has a later date" and "Your local copy has a later date" are gone** from the band under a row. The band keeps the question that follows it: **A real change, or just a rename?**
- **The button that replaces the local file is now labelled "Cloud newer · replace local"** (previously "Cloud newer · download"), and **"Cloud renamed · replace local"** when only the name changed in the cloud. It mirrors **Local newer · replace cloud**: each sits under the side it overwrites.
- **Both buttons are filled in the same circumstances.** After **Check what differs** finds a real change, the button for the newer side is filled in. Before a comparison, **Check what differs** is the filled one on either side.

## Verified

- The full suite passes. A new test renders the real row and checks that the tag is on the newer side only, is absent on a pair that is in step or proven identical, that each button sits under the side it changes, and that both are filled once compared and neither before.
- The tag was drawn in a browser alongside both buttons in all four states (cloud newer and local newer, compared and not compared).
