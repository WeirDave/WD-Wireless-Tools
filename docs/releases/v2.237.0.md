# Quick Walls: Visual Swap can remove all walls on a floor or in the whole project

Suite **2.237.0**. Quick Walls **7.80.0**. Other tools are unchanged.

## What changed

### Quick Walls: Visual Swap

- **A new Remove all walls panel clears every wall on the floor in view, or every wall in the project.** It sits in the sidebar below **Quick swap by type**. **Scope** offers **This floor only** (the default) and **Whole project**. The red button names the scope and the number of walls it will remove, for example *Remove all 42 walls on this floor*. It does not depend on the current selection.
- **The confirmation states the size of the removal.** For **Whole project** it lists the number of walls on each floor, and any walls that sit on no floor.
- **Every wall in scope is removed**, including walls with no wall type, walls whose wall type was deleted, and walls whose geometry could not be drawn.
- **Wall points that no remaining wall uses are removed with the walls**, the same as **Delete selected**.
- **Undo restores the walls and the wall points in their original order.** One **Undo**, or the toolbar undo button, brings back everything a single removal took out. Nothing is written to the file until **Save .esx** is pressed.
- **When the scope holds no walls**, the button is unavailable and reads *No walls on this floor* or *No walls in the whole project*.

## Verified

- The real button is clicked in Firefox, Chrome and Edge on a three-floor project. Removing a floor leaves the other floors untouched; removing the whole project leaves no walls and no wall points; Undo and Redo restore the saved wall and wall-point documents exactly.
- A declined confirmation removes nothing, and the dialog states the count.
- The tests fail when the project scope is made to act on one floor.
- The full suite passes.
