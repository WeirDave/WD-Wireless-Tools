# Prep becomes one workbench: stages on the left, one plan canvas, one Prepare

Suite **2.195.0**, Prep **2.0.0**.

## What changed

### Prep

- **The page is laid out as a workbench.** A rail down the left lists the stages in the order they run: **1 · Trim the canvas**, **2 · Requirement areas**, **3 · Wall types** and **4 · Wall swap**. Each has a tick box to include it and a one-line status, such as "2 of 3 floors cropped · Normal, 10 ft · 1 your box". The plan fills the middle, shared by every stage, with the floors along its top. The current stage's settings sit on the right, and a footer that never scrolls away says what will be written ("Will write: trim 2 floors · requirement areas on 3 floors · 6 wall types added") beside the **Prepare** button.
- **The Trim stage is PlanTrim's box editor.** Drag a rectangle on the plan to choose what to keep, adjust it by its handles or edges, or drag inside it to move it. **Suggest a box**, **Draw my own**, **Apply to all floors** and **Back to automatic** are in the stage's panel, with **Margin around the drawing**. With no box a floor stays automatic, as before. A floor cropped to a drawn box says **Your box** in the floor strip and in the rail.
- **Boxes are shared with PlanTrim.** A box is saved for the project as soon as the drag ends, in the same place PlanTrim keeps its boxes. A box drawn in either tool is used by both, and a box drawn in PlanTrim appears on Prep's plan when the project opens. The "Use the rectangles I drew in PlanTrim" tick box is gone; a box on the plan is the decision.
- **The Areas stage draws each floor's requirement area on the plan**, where it will sit on the cropped sheet, labelled with its people and devices. A floor keeping its existing area shows that area's own outline, dashed.
- **Capture from a project…**, on the Areas stage, reads the device counts out of a finished project and saves them as a capacity template without leaving Prep. **Manage templates** opens Capacity in a new tab. Templates changed in Capacity or Quick Walls are picked up when Prep's tab is shown again.
- **The Wall types stage** lists every type being added or updated, and links to Quick Walls for editing the template.
- **Wall swap** is on the rail. On a project with no walls it is marked unavailable, with the reason. Where walls exist it says how many, and points to Quick Walls' Wall Swap on the prepared copy.
- The plan has **Fit**, **−** and **+** controls and a zoom readout. On every stage but Trim, a plain drag pans the plan.
- **Prepare** is the same one-write pass as before: the stages run trim, then areas, then wall types, and a new copy is written beside the original or downloaded.

## Verified

- The box editor runs in Node on the real plan canvas: a drag draws a box in plan units on an image rendered at twice its size, a click is not a box, a corner resizes, a move stops whole at the edge, and a drag outside starts a new box.
- The page's own handlers run against a stubbed server. The first preview asks for the saved boxes, and after that the page sends its own set. Apply to all copies only to floors of the same size and saves them for PlanTrim. Back to automatic removes one floor's box, Draw my own starts from what automatic keeps, and Prepare sends the boxes the preview showed.
- Through the routes: a box sent crops to itself, an empty set beats a saved box, no request is automatic whatever is saved, and Suggest answers for a dropped project.
- The area outline for each case: kept vertex for vertex, kept when only devices are replaced, the computed rectangle when made or redrawn, and none for a floor left alone.
- Driven in headless Chromium against the real server with a synthetic two-floor project: open, draw a box by dragging on the canvas, apply to all, switch stages, Prepare. The file was written and there were no console errors.
- The full test suite passes.
