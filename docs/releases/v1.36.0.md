**Quick Walls.** The wall list now splits into two groups — **Standard Ekahau** and **Custom Walls** — so your own additions don't get lost in the middle of the factory set. Classification is based on whether a wall type's key exists in the bundled `ekahau_defaults.json`; each group stays alphabetically sorted internally. Also added a new custom wall type to the bundled WD Template: **Walls, Steel 12ft** — a solid steel enclosure/cage profile (400 dB/m attenuation across all bands, 0.405 reflection, 0.1m thickness) for bolted-down steel structures, distinct from the existing open-frame Warehouse Rack Wall types.
