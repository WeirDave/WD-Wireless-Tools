WD Cloud Manager — complete A-Z alphabet in the ledger. Every letter renders whether it has sites or not, giving the jump-nav a target for every click and giving late letters enough content depth to actually reach the top of the viewport.

Suite `v1.9.26 → v1.9.27`. WD Cloud Manager `v4.6.2 → v4.6.3`.

## The gap the maintainer hit

Two problems from v1.9.26's jump-nav:

1. **Clicking W (or any late letter) didn't scroll to the top on tall monitors.** `scrollTop` is capped at `scrollHeight - clientHeight`, and there wasn't enough content below W (only ~200px of WLHM1 sites) to let W scroll all the way up. It reached the top of what it could reach, which wasn't the top of the viewport.

2. **First attempted fix (`padding-bottom: 80vh` on the rows container) was a band-aid.** the maintainer called it correctly. Empty padding at the bottom is arbitrary space that solves the symptom without informing the user of anything. Reverted.

## The real fix — always render the full alphabet

Both the Sites tree view (`renderSitesTree`) and the flat projects view (`renderLedger`) now iterate ALL 27 letter buckets (`#` + A–Z) and emit a header for every one — even the ones with no sites. Empty headers get an `.empty-letter` class that dims them to 35% opacity and stops the pointer, so they read as "nothing yet" rather than "click me and something happens."

Effects:

- **Every jump-nav click has a target.** No dead clicks even when the current view has gaps in the alphabet.
- **Users see the shape of what's missing.** Scrolling past the dimmed letters is a visual reminder of "we don't have any T sites in the Others filter" without needing to check a filter or search.
- **Scroll depth is real, not padded.** Each empty header contributes ~50px of natural content, so by the time you get to Z there's enough content above and below for the scroll to put any letter at the top. No arbitrary `80vh`.

## Verified in the preview

Ran the whole alphabet through `_jumpToLetter` after the change:

- A → scrollTop=182, target 36px from container top ✓
- P → scrollTop=5462, target 36px from container top ✓
- W → scrollTop=8324, target 36px from container top ✓
- Z → scrollTop=8622 (well below max of 9205), target 36px from container top ✓

Every letter — including the last — reaches the same 36px offset (the auto-assign banner is what sits above; that's the actual page-top for the ledger).

DOM verified: 27 headers rendered, 11 marked `.empty-letter` (`#`, E, G, J, K, N, Q, R, X, Y, Z in the current Mine filter), 16 with real content.
